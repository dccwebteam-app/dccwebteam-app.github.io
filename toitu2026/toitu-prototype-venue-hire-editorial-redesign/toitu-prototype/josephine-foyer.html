The space

Character in
every detail.

With floor-to-ceiling glass overlooking Dunedin’s heritage district, the Josephine Foyer offers a generous and memorable backdrop for gatherings.

Heart Rimu floors, Oamaru Stone and locally sourced Bluestone give the space a distinctly Otago character.

From seated dinners and presentations to receptions and celebrations, the flexible layout can be arranged around your event.
