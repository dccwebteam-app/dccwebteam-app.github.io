Research

Explore Otago and Southland family history, photographs, passenger records and other archive resources through the Toitū Research Centre.

Research Centre

Start your research

We offer self-guided research through our Research Centre, which is open from 10am to 5pm daily.

If you are researching your family history, we recommend reading the detailed Research Guide first, as many resources are now available online.

Download the Research Guide ↓

Available resources

Explore the archive

The Research Centre provides access to a wide range of resources for family, local and social history research.

01

OASES

Search the Otago and Southland Early Settlers database, a digital genealogy catalogue drawing on more than 300 primary sources and records for more than 160,000 people.

02

Portraits

Search a manual index of more than 7,000 photographic portraits held in the Archive. Ask a Visitor Host to view digital images from the index.

03

Early Dunedin photographs

Explore more than 4,000 photographic prints organised by subjects including streets, suburbs, transport and public events.

04

Passenger lists

Access indexed transcripts of newspaper shipping columns and lists of assisted immigrants to Otago.

05

Military

Find pathways to military records and the Dunedin Roll of Honour compiled by Toitū from a wide variety of sources.

06

Thomas Burns’ visitation book

View a copy of the earliest informal census created by Reverend Thomas Burns, recording residents of Dunedin from 1848 to 1858.

07

Reference library

Browse the Research Centre’s selection of books on local history.

08

Railways

View a selection of railway images from the Rockliff and Emerson collections.
