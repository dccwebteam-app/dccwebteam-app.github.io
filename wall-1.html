Wall 01

Arrivals 1820s – 40s

Discover the people who arrived in Otago before the end of 1849.

The portraits on this wall are of people who arrived in Otago up to the end of 1849. The top row features large oil paintings, including the leaders of the 1848 settlers, Captain William Cargill and Reverend Thomas Burns, and their wives.

Photographs on the following rows are arranged by arrival date, with husbands and wives kept together even when they arrived on different ships. The sequence begins with Edwin Palmer, who arrived in 1825.

This is a selection from a much larger collection. More portraits can be accessed through the Museum’s Research Centre.
