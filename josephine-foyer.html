Josephine Foyer

An inviting space for receptions, celebrations and events in the heart of Toitū.

The space

Gather somewhere special.

The Josephine Foyer brings together heritage materials and contemporary architecture, with generous glazing and views towards Dunedin's historic precinct.

Its flexible layout accommodates everything from seated dinners to presentations and receptions.
